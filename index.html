Delivery and pickup policy

You can place an order online for pickup or, where offered, delivery. Choose your option at checkout.

Pickup

Orders are prepared fresh. We will show an estimated ready time when you check out, usually [15–30] minutes. Pick up your order at the address above and bring your order confirmation.

All orders are food prepared to order. No items are shipped by mail or carrier.

Refund policy

We want you to be happy with your order. Because food is prepared fresh and cannot be resold, all sales are final once an order has been prepared, with the exceptions below.

  • Order problem: if an item is missing, incorrect, or there is a quality problem, contact us the same day. We will remake the item or refund the affected item.
  • Cancellation: an order can be cancelled for a full refund if you contact us before preparation has started.
  • Order we cannot fulfill: if we cancel your order for any reason, you will receive a full refund.
  • Duplicate or incorrect charge: contact us and we will correct it.

Approved refunds are returned to the original card. Your bank may take 3 to 10 business days to show the credit.

Acknowledgement: by placing an order, you confirm that you have read and agree to this refund policy and to the terms and conditions below.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address (for delivery), and the details of your order. Payment card details are entered on our secure payment processor's form and are not stored by the restaurant.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare and deliver your order, send order confirmations and updates, resolve problems, and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run your order, such as our online ordering platform, payment processor and, where applicable, delivery staff, or when the law requires it.

Security and retention

Payments are processed over encrypted connections. We keep order records only as long as needed for business and legal purposes.
